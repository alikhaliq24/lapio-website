lapio sends personalized review requests via SMS and email on your behalf. Once you connect your CRM or import a customer list, we automatically trigger messages after each job completes. Every message includes your customer's name and a one-tap link directly to your Google review page.

Asking customers for reviews is completely allowed by Google. What Google prohibits is offering incentives or selectively targeting only customers you expect will rate you positively. lapio asks all customers equally and never filters by expected sentiment, keeping you fully compliant.

We integrate natively with Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks, ServiceTitan, and Workiz. We also support Zapier, which connects lapio to thousands of additional tools. If your CRM isn't listed, reach out. We can usually build a custom integration quickly.

Most customers see their first new reviews within 48 hours. Meaningful rating improvement typically follows within 30–60 days. Your conversion rate depends on message timing and personalization, which our onboarding call is specifically designed to optimize for your business.
